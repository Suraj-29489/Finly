# Phase 7 - Recurring Expenses

## Objective

Add support for recurring expenses such as rent, subscriptions, bills, memberships, and other regular payments.

## What We Build

Recurring transaction scheduler, subscription tracking, and automatic expense generation for regular payments.

## Expected Result

Regular expenses can be managed without repeatedly entering them manually.
